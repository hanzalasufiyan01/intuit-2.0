# Using the assistant

The assistant answers questions about how Intuit 2.0 works. Open it with the **Assistant** button at the top of any page, or with **Ask the assistant** in the Help centre.

## What it can do

- Explain how to do something, with the menu names and buttons you will see, for example how to create and issue an invoice or what the Trial Balance shows.
- Point you to the related tutorial.
- Tell you your own totals when you ask, for example "How much do customers owe us?", "What are our unpaid bills?" or "What is our net profit this year?". These appear in a **From your books** block with the date or period and where the same figures live in the application.

## What it cannot do

- It cannot create, change, post, pay, refund or delete anything. It only explains; you do the action yourself.
- It cannot file tax returns, run statutory payroll, fetch bank feeds or scan receipts: those are not part of the application.
- It only shows figures you are allowed to see. If your role cannot view a report, the assistant says the figure is not available to you.
- It does not invent numbers. If a figure cannot be produced (for example accounting is not set up yet), it says so.

## Two modes

The panel tells you which one your organization's server uses:

- **Built-in help** answers directly from the Intuit 2.0 help catalogue.
- **AI-assisted wording** has a language model rephrase the same catalogue content and your verified figures. It is only labelled this way when it is actually switched on. If the AI service cannot answer, you get the built-in answer with a notice.

Your administrator chooses the mode (see `assistant.md`).

## Tips

- Ask one thing at a time. Press **Ctrl+Enter** to send.
- Use the suggested questions to get started.
- **Clear conversation** starts over. The conversation is kept only in your browser while you are signed in to the current organization. It is not saved on the server, and it is cleared when you sign out or switch organization.
- Do not type passwords, card numbers or other secrets.
- If you ask too quickly, the assistant tells you how many seconds to wait.
- For anything that affects your books, check the figure in the report named in the answer.
