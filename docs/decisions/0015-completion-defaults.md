# ADR 0015: Completion defaults for features the master specification names but does not define

Status: **ACCEPTED LOCALLY as reversible defaults** (2026-10-19). The product owner asked for the remaining master-specification features to be built wherever a reasonable, reversible default is supported by the specification, without inventing tax law, statutory payroll rates, clinical policy or accounting treatment. This record lists each default, what it deliberately does NOT do, and what would change it. Nothing here is legal advice. The owner can reverse any item; each is isolated in its own tables and routes.

## Principles used to choose a default

1. **Side tables, not edits.** New behaviour is added in new tables so that frozen modules (customers, items, invoices, inventory ledger) keep their meaning and their tests.
2. **Advisory before blocking.** Where the specification does not say what must be prevented, the default records and reports; it does not block a sale or a posting. Blocking exists only where the organization itself defines the rule (expense policies).
3. **The organization supplies every number and rule.** No limit, rate, period, wording or account is built in.
4. **Nothing posts to the ledger unless the specification or the owner says it does.** Where something posts (petty cash differences), it goes through the accounting event path to an account the organization chose.
5. **Append-only or archivable.** Records are archived or voided, never deleted; final records are enforced by database triggers.

## Still genuinely the owner's decision

Listed with the exact question in `docs/open-decisions.md` and tracked row by row in `docs/master-spec-coverage.md`: approval gating of exports and permission changes; compound and multiple taxes; fixed-asset methods beyond straight-line and impairment accounting; custom fields on patients, vendors and items; clinical records and scheduling extras; named role templates; consolidation; employment types and attendance; bank-feed, OCR and SMS providers; a real payment provider; customer and vendor portal sign-in; every MIRA-dependent item; payroll statutory rules; Dhivehi translation.

## How to reverse a default

Each table above is independent. To remove a feature: stop registering its routes in `apps/api/src/api/v1/index.ts` and its services in `app.ts`, remove its web route, and add a forward-only migration that drops its tables (applied migrations are checksum-protected and are never edited). Because the defaults only add side tables and never change the posting engine, no ledger data depends on them except petty cash count journals (which are ordinary posted journals).
