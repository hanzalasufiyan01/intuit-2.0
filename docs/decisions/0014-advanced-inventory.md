# ADR 0014 — Advanced inventory (warehouses, transfers, lots/serials/expiry, landed cost, units of measure)

Status: decided 2026-10-19 by the implementer under the standing completion mandate ("resolve costing or authorization decisions before implementing"); every decision below is reversible before the stage that uses it ships and is listed in `docs/open-decisions.md` for the owner's veto. It builds on ADR 0006 (weighted-average cost, immutable stock ledger) and ADR 0012 (returns). The original master specification is not in the repository; these decisions come from `docs/design/advanced-inventory.md` and the ADRs.

## Decisions

- **AI-01 Costing stays global per item.** One weighted-average cost per item across all warehouses and lots (ADR 0006 I-03 is unchanged). Warehouses and lots carry QUANTITIES; value stays on the item. Reason: inventory accounts are per item, so per-warehouse cost would need per-warehouse ledger accounts or a split of every journal, which is how stock and general ledger drift apart. A warehouse's value is shown as quantity times the item's current average and is informational (rounding differences are not booked). Lot-level (FIFO/specific) costing is NOT built.
- **AI-02 One placement ledger.** `inventory_stock_entries` is an immutable ledger of signed quantities by (item, warehouse, optional lot). Every stock movement writes entries that sum to the movement's quantity; transfers write paired entries with no value movement. `inventory_stock_levels` (warehouse) and `inventory_lot_levels` (lot) are maintained by database triggers from the entries. A deferred constraint trigger refuses to commit unless, for every item, the sum of its warehouse levels equals `inventory_balances.quantity` (and, for lot-tracked items, the sum of lot levels does too). A bug in any posting path therefore fails the transaction instead of corrupting stock.
- **AI-03 Default warehouse.** Every organization with tracked items has exactly one default warehouse (`MAIN`, created by the migration or on first use). Existing movements are backfilled into it entry by entry. A code path that does not name a warehouse behaves exactly as before.
- **AI-04 Where the warehouse is chosen.** Documents already in the engine (invoices, bills, vendor credits) are not given new header fields. A draft document's warehouse is stored in `inventory_document_locations` (set while the document is a draft, read when stock moves; approvals keep it). With no choice, stock IN goes to the default warehouse and stock OUT is allocated across warehouses, default first. Adjustments, customer returns, counts and transfers name their warehouse in their own request. Voids reverse the exact entries of the movement they reverse, never a fresh choice.
- **AI-05 Negative stock per warehouse** follows the organization policy (block by default). Blocking is enforced per warehouse and per lot, not only per item.
- **AI-06 Transfers** (`TRF-`) move quantity between two active warehouses; no journal, no value movement (both warehouses use the item's inventory account). A transfer can be voided only while the destination still holds the quantity (and lot). Permissions reuse `inventory.adjustments.create` / `.void`; warehouse management uses `inventory.settings.manage`.
- **AI-07 Lots, serials and expiry** (stage B). An item's tracking mode is `none`, `lot` or `serial`, with an optional expiry flag. A lot is unique per item by code, with an optional expiry date. A serial is a lot of quantity one. Stock IN of a lot/serial item must name its lots (bill lines via the document-lot side table, adjustment lines inline). Stock OUT picks automatically: earliest expiry first (no expiry last), then oldest; expired lots are skipped for sales and can be written off with an adjustment; a sale that cannot be covered by unexpired lots is refused. Choosing specific serials on a sale is NOT built; the serials that left are recorded and shown. Lot-level costing is not built (AI-01).
- **AI-08 Landed cost** (stage C). A landed-cost document (`LC-`) references one posted bill, a cost amount, a credit (clearing) account and an allocation basis (value or quantity) across the bill's tracked lines. For each item the share attributable to units still on hand is added to inventory value (a value-only movement); the share attributable to units already sold goes to that item's cost of goods sold. Posting is one journal through an accounting event; voiding reverses the journal and the movements. The amount is booked from the credit account the user chooses (typically a freight/duty accrual that a vendor bill clears); the module never invents a tax or duty treatment.
- **AI-09 Units of measure** (stage D). An item has a base unit (the stock unit) and optional purchase and sales units with a conversion factor to the base unit. When an item has a purchase unit, bill and vendor-credit line quantities of that item are in that unit; with a sales unit, invoice and credit-note line quantities are. Stock quantity = line quantity times factor, which must be exact to six decimals (otherwise the line is refused: no silent rounding). Unit prices stay per line unit. Adjustments, counts, transfers, returns and stock reports are in the base unit. Items without units behave exactly as before.
- **AI-10 Invariants** every stage must keep and test: item stock value equals the inventory accounts' ledger balance (valuation difference zero); item quantity equals the sum of warehouse quantities and of lot quantities; the stock ledger stays append-only; every posting is idempotent, period checked and audited; tenant isolation by row-level security on every new table.

## Not decided here (owner input welcome, nothing depends on it)

Per-warehouse cost, lot-level costing, selecting specific serials on sale, warehouse-level approval rules, bin/shelf locations within a warehouse, reservations and pick lists.
