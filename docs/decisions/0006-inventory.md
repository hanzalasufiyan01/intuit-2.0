# ADR 0006 — Inventory

Status: implemented (migration 0046). Architecture approved 2026-10-07: weighted-average cost, negative stock blocked by default, immutable stock movements and valuation data, Sales cost of goods sold as an additive integration (the Sales posting is not rewritten), no goods-received-not-invoiced (GRNI) account in v1.

## Decisions

- **I-01 Tracked items.** An item becomes stock-tracked by a row in `inventory_items` that names its inventory asset account and its cost-of-goods-sold account. The shared items catalog is not changed except that tracking sets the item's purchase defaults (`isPurchased`, expense account = the inventory account). Only active products can be tracked; the inventory account is an other (current) asset account (leaf, active, not a control or designated account) and the cost account an expense account. An item that never moved can be untracked; once it has stock history it stays tracked and its accounts are fixed (database trigger).
- **I-02 Stock ledger.** `inventory_movements` is the immutable stock ledger (the database refuses UPDATE and DELETE): each row keeps the signed quantity, the signed base value, the quantity and value after it, the source document and the journal. A document moves an item at most once per movement kind (unique index), so a replay never double-counts. `inventory_balances` holds the running quantity and base value per item and changes only together with a movement, under a row lock taken in item order.
- **I-03 Costing.** Perpetual weighted average. A receipt adds its quantity and base value; an issue takes the average cost, and the issue that empties the stock takes the whole remaining value (no residue). Values are in base-currency minor units. A void restores or removes stock at the cost of the movement it reverses, not at the current average. Costing is in posting order: a document dated in the past does not re-cost later movements.
- **I-04 Stock in: bills.** A bill that carries a tracked item brings stock in when it posts, at the base cost the bill's own journal booked to the inventory account (net plus capitalized tax, allocated across items so the stock value equals the ledger exactly). The bill posting is unchanged; Inventory is called inside its transaction. A tracked item's bill line must post to its inventory account, and an account that holds tracked stock accepts tracked items only (validation at post). Voiding a bill removes its stock and is refused while that stock has been used. No GRNI: stock enters on the bill, not on a receiving document.
- **I-05 Stock out: invoices.** Issuing an invoice that carries a tracked item takes the stock out at the average cost and posts Dr cost of goods sold / Cr inventory through the `inventory.cogs_posted` accounting event, in the same transaction, base currency, with the invoice line dimensions. The invoice's own journal is not touched. Voiding the invoice returns the stock at the original cost and reverses the cost journal through Inventory. Issuing is refused (`409 INSUFFICIENT_STOCK`, nothing issued) when it would take stock below zero.
- **I-06 Negative stock.** `block` by default; an organization can `allow` it (`inventory.settings.manage` and a recent password confirmation). With negative stock allowed, the part of an issue beyond the stock on hand is costed at the last unit cost. Blocking again is refused while any item is negative. The balance row carries the policy it was written under and the database refuses a negative quantity or value when blocked.
- **I-07 Adjustments.** A stock adjustment (`ADJ-`) is an accounting source document: lines of signed quantity change, an increase costed at a stated unit cost (or the average) and a decrease at the average. It posts Dr/Cr the item's inventory account against the offset account (organization setting or per adjustment) through `inventory.adjustment_posted`; the void reverses the journal and the movements (refused when the stock has since been used). Each item once per adjustment.
- **I-08 Journal ownership.** `JournalOwner` now covers Inventory: the generic journal reversal refuses an Inventory journal; only the invoice void or the adjustment void reverses it.
- **I-09 Valuation.** `GET /inventory/valuation` lists stock per item and compares the stock value per inventory account with the general ledger balance of that account; a manual journal into an inventory account shows as a difference.
- **I-10 Permissions.** `inventory.view`, `inventory.items.manage`, `inventory.settings.manage`, `inventory.adjustments.create`, `inventory.adjustments.void`. Owner and Administrator hold all; Member holds the view key. Migration 0046 backfills existing organizations additively with audit events.

## Known deviations and open points

- **Credit notes and vendor credits do not move stock yet.** A credit note for returned goods reverses revenue and receivable but neither restores stock nor reverses cost of goods sold; record a stock adjustment. Tracked items on vendor credits and direct expenses are refused with a message rather than posted without a stock effect.
- **Inventory accounts are not control accounts.** A manual journal can still post to one; the valuation report shows the difference. Making them subledger control accounts (like AR and AP) needs a change to the control-account constraint and is left for the architects.
- **One stock location, no units of measure, lots or serials.** Quantities are in the item's unit.
- **Approvals.** Cost-of-goods-sold and adjustment journals post under the permissions of the document that causes them, without a separate approval step.
- **Opening stock** is entered with a stock adjustment (increase with a unit cost) against the opening-balance offset account.
