# ADR 0007 — Purchase orders, goods receiving and three-way matching

Status: implemented (migration 0047). Architecture approved 2026-10-07: purchase orders create no journal; no goods-received-not-invoiced (GRNI) account in v1; three-way matching is configurable per organization as OFF, WARNING or BLOCK with a quantity and a price tolerance; the order lifecycle is DRAFT, PENDING_APPROVAL, APPROVED, PARTIALLY_RECEIVED, RECEIVED, CLOSED, CANCELLED.

## Decisions

- **PO-01 An order is not an accounting document.** It commits to a vendor for quantities and prices. It never posts, never touches AP, and does not appear in the AP aging. Its lines use the bill line columns (tax code and recoverability, discounts, accounts, dimensions) and the shared purchase resolution, so an order is priced, taxed and validated like a bill; they also carry the quantity received and billed so far.
- **PO-02 Lifecycle.** DRAFT is editable and deletable. Submit recomputes the order with today's references, assigns the `PO-` number (kept if the order is returned to draft) and either opens an approval request (action `purchases.order.approve`, type `purchase_order`, the base amount at the order-date rate; no rate fails amount conditions closed) or, when no policy step matches, approves the order at once. The approval itself moves the order to APPROVED; a rejection (comment required) or a withdrawal returns it to DRAFT; no self-approval. From APPROVED the status follows the quantities received (PARTIALLY_RECEIVED, RECEIVED, and back when a receipt is voided). CLOSED (reason optional) and CANCELLED (reason required; only an approved order with nothing received or billed) are terminal. The commercial terms are fixed after submission (database trigger); only the lifecycle columns and the received and billed quantities change.
- **PO-03 Goods receipts.** A goods receipt (`GR-`) records quantities against order lines of an approved order. A line may receive up to its ordered quantity plus the quantity tolerance. A receipt never posts (no GRNI). Voiding a receipt takes its quantities back, refused when a line has been billed beyond what would then remain received. Receipts are immutable.
- **PO-04 Bills.** A bill is linked to at most one order of the same vendor and currency while it is a draft (`POST .../link-bill`), or created from the order (`POST .../create-bill`): the lines are the quantities still to be billed (what was received, or what was ordered) at the order prices; a document discount is carried only when every line is billed in full. A linked draft bill can be deleted with its link.
- **PO-05 Three-way match.** At bill post, each bill line is matched to the order lines of the same item (an item-less line by its description) and allocated across them in line order. Price: the bill's unit price after the line discount against the order's, within the price tolerance. Quantity: the total billed on an order line (with this bill) against what was received and against what was ordered, each within the quantity tolerance. A bill line that is not on the order is flagged. BLOCK refuses the post with every issue listed (nothing is posted); WARNING posts and records the issues; OFF does not check but still applies the billed quantities. The result is kept with the posted bill (`purchase_bill_matches`, immutable), an audit event is written, and a void of the bill takes its billed quantities off the order lines. A bill cannot post against a closed or cancelled order.
- **PO-06 Additive.** Purchases is called, not rewritten: the bill service calls `checkBill` before posting and `recordBill` after, and `releaseBill` on void (`PurchaseOrderHooks`); the bill posting and its journal are unchanged.
- **PO-07 Commitments.** `GET /purchases/reports/commitments` lists, per vendor and currency, what is ordered and what is still to be billed on approved orders (open value = line total × the unbilled share).
- **PO-08 Permissions.** `purchase_orders.view|create|approve|receive|close|settings.manage`. Owner and Administrator hold all; Member holds the view key. Migration 0047 backfills existing organizations additively with audit events. Orders accept attachments (file link type `purchase_order`), removable only while the order is a draft.
- **PO-09 Numbering.** `PO-` and `GR-` come from `purchase_order_number_sequences` (five digits), created on first use.

## Known deviations and open points

- Order totals are in the order currency; the commitments report does not convert to base.
- A line cannot be matched to two different orders; a bill has one order.
- Receiving does not move stock: with Inventory, stock still enters when the bill posts (no GRNI).
- Quantities match by item or exact description only; there is no unit-of-measure conversion.
- There is no separate override permission for a BLOCK mismatch: correct the bill or the order, receive the goods, or change the mode.
