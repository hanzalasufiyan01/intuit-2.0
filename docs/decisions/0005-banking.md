# ADR 0005 — Banking

Status: implemented (migration 0045). Architecture approved 2026-10-07: banking is a separate module, CSV and OFX sit behind a provider abstraction, bank reconciliation never modifies posted journal lines, and a transfer is a proper accounting source transaction.

## Decisions

- **B-01 Bank account.** A banking account follows one bank, cash or credit-card posting account of the chart (leaf, active, not a control account). Its currency is the chart account's. One banking account per chart account. Opening statement balance and its date anchor the first reconciliation and are fixed once statements are imported.
- **B-02 Statement providers.** A provider turns a statement into plain transactions and touches no tables (`StatementProvider`). CSV uses a user-chosen column mapping (single signed amount or debit and credit columns, three date formats, decimal point or comma, delimiter auto-detected); OFX reads 1.x SGML and 2.x XML with plain patterns (no entity or DTD resolution; a `<!DOCTYPE`/`<!ENTITY` is refused). A live-feed provider can implement the same interface later. Limits: 5 MB, 25,000 rows. A statement with any unreadable row is refused whole, with row numbers; nothing is partly imported.
- **B-03 Statement transactions are facts.** Signed from the account's side (positive is money in). Rows are immutable (database trigger); only the matching status changes. Never deleted.
- **B-04 Duplicate detection.** A fingerprint of date, amount, normalized text and reference, numbered by occurrence within a file, is unique per account; the bank's own transaction id (OFX FITID) is unique per account when given. Re-importing an overlapping statement skips what the account holds and reports the count. Two genuinely identical lines in one file are both kept.
- **B-05 Matching.** A match is a row in `banking_matches` pointing at a posted journal line of the account; it never sets a flag on or changes a journal line. One active match per transaction and per line; unmatching ends the match and keeps the row. A database trigger requires the line to be on the account's chart account, posted, and of exactly the transaction's amount (own currency for a foreign account, base amount for a base-currency account). Auto-match matches only a transaction that has exactly one candidate line within three days.
- **B-06 Bank entry (categorize).** Categorizing an unmatched transaction posts Dr/Cr the bank account against a chosen account through the `banking.entry_posted` accounting event (document → event → journal) and matches the transaction at once. The category is an active posting account that is not a control account and not itself a bank account (a move between your own accounts is a transfer). Numbers `BE-`. Dimensions are validated and required dimensions enforced like every other posting.
- **B-07 Transfers.** A transfer posts Dr destination / Cr source through `banking.transfer_posted`, numbered `TR-`. Same currency: one amount (a table rate is required for a foreign currency). One base-currency and one foreign account: both amounts are entered and the journal is in the foreign currency at the implied rate (10 decimals; amounts that a 10-decimal rate cannot reproduce within 0.01 are refused); the base-side amount stored is exactly what the journal booked. **Limitation:** a transfer between two accounts in different foreign currencies is not supported in v1.
- **B-08 Voids.** A bank entry or transfer is voided by reversing its journal through Banking (`banking.transfers.void` and a recent password confirmation). `JournalOwner` extends the subledger rule: the generic journal reversal refuses a Banking journal (`SYSTEM_JOURNAL`). A void is refused while a reconciliation counts one of its transactions; otherwise its matches end and the transactions become unmatched again. A voided entry's transaction can be categorized again.
- **B-09 Reconciliation.** Compares the matched (cleared) transactions up to the statement date, not yet counted by an earlier reconciliation, with the statement balance: opening (the previous statement's closing, or the account's starting balance) + cleared = closing. It completes only with a difference of zero, for a date after the previous one, and writes only its own tables (`banking_reconciliations`, `banking_reconciliation_items`); a test proves the ledger lines are byte-identical before and after. A reconciled transaction cannot be unmatched, excluded or voided. Reopening (`banking.reconcile` and re-authentication, reason required) is allowed for the latest reconciliation only; its items stay as history (`counted = false`).
- **B-10 Rules.** A rule (direction, description contains, amount range, optional bank account, priority) suggests a category for an unmatched transaction. Rules never post.
- **B-11 Numbering.** `BE-` and `TR-` sequences live in `banking_number_sequences`, created on first use with defaults (five digits).
- **B-12 Permissions.** Nine keys (`banking.accounts.manage`, `banking.transactions.view|import|match|categorize`, `banking.transfers.create|void`, `banking.reconcile`, `banking.rules.manage`). Owner and Administrator hold all; Member holds the view key. Migration 0045 backfills existing organizations additively with audit events.
- **B-13 Audit and idempotency.** Every state change writes an append-only audit event (`bank_account.*`, `bank_statement.imported`, `bank_transaction.*`, `bank_entry.*`, `bank_transfer.*`, `bank_reconciliation.*`, `bank_rule.*`). Statement import, categorize and transfer creation accept `Idempotency-Key` and replay the first result.

## Known deviations and open points

- **Approvals.** Bank entries and transfers post under their permissions without the journal approval workflow (`domainApproval: true`, like Purchases). A conditional approval step for them is a decision for the architects.
- **Void clutter.** A voided entry leaves the original and the reversal line both unmatched in the register (net zero).
- **Foreign ↔ foreign transfers** between different non-base currencies are refused.
- **Statements by JSON.** Statements are posted as JSON text (`content`), not uploaded as files, because OFX is not a detected file type; the route body limit is raised to 6 MB for the import routes only. Attachments to banking documents are not offered in v1.
- **Credit-card accounts** are bank accounts like any other; a statement amount is signed from the ledger account's debit side (a card purchase is money out).
