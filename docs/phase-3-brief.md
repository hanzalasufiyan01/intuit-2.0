# Intuit 2.0 — Phase 3 Architecture & Implementation Brief v2.4

- **Status:** APPROVED / FROZEN through **Decision 92**. The decisions are in [ADR 0003](decisions/0003-phase-3-sales-receivables.md); FX journal changes are in [ADR 0002 Amendment 1](decisions/0002-phase-2-accounting.md#amendment-1-phase-3--fx-journal-architecture-and-related-changes). The Phase 3A work plan is [phase-3a-implementation-plan.md](phase-3a-implementation-plan.md).
- **Supersedes:** brief v1.0 (draft), v2.0, v2.1, v2.2 and v2.3. v2.0 placed idempotency and i18n in 3A and listed P1–P13 as proposals.
- **Structure (Decision 19):** Phase 3A Foundations → Checkpoint 3A → one local commit; then Phase 3B Sales & AR → Checkpoint 3B → one local commit.
- **Model:** modular monolith. PostgreSQL is the single database. The ledger is the only financial source of truth.
- **Governance:** Proposal → Review → Approval → Freeze → Implementation. Conflicts are reported and implementation stops before the conflicting change.

---

## 1. Objective

Deliver QuickBooks Online / Xero-class foundations (Phase 3A) and a complete Sales & Accounts Receivable area on top of them (Phase 3B), without narrowing functional scope.

## 2. Frozen foundations and supersessions

- Phase 1 and Phase 2 are frozen except for the supersessions in ADR 0003 and ADR 0002 Amendment 1 (A1.1–A1.7).
- Migrations `0001`–`0003` are never edited. Phase 3 adds `0004+`.
- The full Phase 1 + Phase 2 test suite stays green at every checkpoint.

## 10. Accounting integration patterns

- **Invoice:** Dr AR control / Cr revenue (net of pro-rata discount) / Cr tax (snapshot).
- **Receipt:** Dr deposit (receipt rate) / Cr AR at historical base / `base_only` FX line.
- **Credit note:** the reverse of the invoice. **Voids:** reversals.
- **Opening balances:** one journal per currency in one batch, with differences to Opening Balance Equity; no AR control lines in 3A.
- **Revaluation (engine only):** `base_only` adjustments on foreign monetary accounts and open documents, against Unrealized FX, with reversal.
- **Every system journal:** carries a source reference and dimensions (Decision 16) and posts atomically (C1, Decision 13).
- **Invariant:** the AR subledger equals the AR control balance.
