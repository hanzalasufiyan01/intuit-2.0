## Clinic (ADR 0013 open inputs)

DECIDED by the owner on 2026-10-19: consent is a register with organization-entered wording (no gating); retention is an organization-set number of years with a manual review list (no automatic removal); no third-party payer billing; no patient access. STILL OPEN (owner's input, not mine to invent): the legal retention period itself, the consent wording, whether clinical notes may ever be stored (needs a new ADR). The module stores administrative and billing data only, is off by default and is not exposed through the public API.

## Master specification: decisions needed from the owner (2026-10-19)

Each row is something the master specification names but does not define. Nothing below has been built or guessed. The recommendation is a suggestion only; it is not applied until the owner says so. Full status per bullet: `docs/master-spec-coverage.md`.

| Topic                                                              | Exact decision needed                                                                                                                                                               | Suggested default (not applied)                                                               |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Expense by employee (spec section 16)                              | Whether "employee" means claims by claimant, payroll cost per employee, or expenses attributed to an employee by a new field or dimension                                           | Claims by claimant and payroll cost by employee as two separate, labelled views; never summed |
| Approval for data exports                                          | Which exports need approval (all, or above a size/sensitivity), what the requester sees while waiting, who is notified, whether the export file is created before or after approval | Only exports of payroll and clinic data; created after approval                               |
| Approval for permission changes                                    | Which changes (role permission edits, role assignment, ownership), what stays in force while pending                                                                                | Role permission edits; the old permissions stay until approved                                |
| Expense policies (APPLIED as a reversible default, ADR 0015 D-09)  | The rules: per-category/per-role limits, receipt required above an amount, allowed accounts; whether a breach blocks, warns or goes to approval                                     | Receipt required above an amount; breach goes to the existing approval engine                 |
| Petty cash (APPLIED per owner answer, ADR 0015 D-10)               | A float with a custodian and top-up/reimbursement, or simply a cash account                                                                                                         | A cash account with a custodian and a count-and-reconcile step                                |
| Delivery stage (APPLIED as a reversible default, ADR 0015 D-07)    | Is delivery its own document; does it issue stock and post cost of sales (moving cost of sales from the invoice to the delivery) or only record fulfilment                          | Optional delivery note that records fulfilment only; cost stays on the invoice                |
| Stock reservations (APPLIED as an advisory default, ADR 0015 D-08) | What reserves stock (sales order, quote, manual); when released; whether reserved stock is unavailable to sales and transfers                                                       | Sales orders reserve; reserved stock cannot be sold or transferred                            |
| Product variants (APPLIED as a reversible default, ADR 0015 D-05)  | A variant as its own item under a parent, or an option set on one item; how attributes are defined; price, cost and stock per variant                                               | Each variant is an item (own SKU, barcode, stock) grouped under a parent                      |
| Internal locations (APPLIED as labels, ADR 0015 D-06)              | Bins inside a warehouse: stock tracked per bin, or a label on the movement                                                                                                          | Stock per bin inside a warehouse, with transfers between bins                                 |
| FIFO costing                                                       | Whether FIFO is needed at all (the owner chose weighted average, ADR 0014 AI-01) and how it would coexist per item                                                                  | Keep weighted average                                                                         |
| Compound and multiple taxes                                        | Order (tax on net, or on net plus the first tax), rounding per tax or per line, how several taxes show on documents and in returns                                                  | Tax on net per line, each tax rounded per line, listed separately                             |
| Fixed-asset depreciation methods                                   | Which methods beyond straight-line, their parameters (rate or factor, switch to straight line), first-period treatment; impairment and write-off accounting                         | Declining balance with an entered rate; impairment as an expense with a note                  |
| Custom fields beyond customers                                     | Which records (patients, vendors, items), whether a field can be required or appear on documents                                                                                    | Customers only (built); vendors and items next                                                |
| Clinical records                                                   | Whether session notes, treatment plans or assessments are kept; who may read them; retention; a separate store and audit policy                                                     | Not stored until a new ADR defines it                                                         |
| Clinic scheduling extras                                           | Practitioner availability, rooms/resources, recurring appointments, reminders (and what may be sent to patients)                                                                    | Availability and rooms first; no patient messages                                             |
| Named role templates                                               | Receptionist, Sales Staff, Inventory Manager, Auditor: the permission set of each                                                                                                   | Provide none; owners build custom roles                                                       |
| Parent/subsidiary and consolidation                                | The parent link, the reporting currency and rate source, intercompany identification and elimination                                                                                | See the legal-entity row above                                                                |
| Employment types and attendance                                    | The list of employment types and what each changes; whether attendance is recorded in the system                                                                                    | A free label only; no attendance                                                              |
| Bank feeds, OCR, SMS providers                                     | Which provider for each, and whether an interface is wanted before a provider is chosen                                                                                             | Wait for a provider choice                                                                    |
| Vendor and customer portal sign-in                                 | Sign-in model for outside parties (e-mail link, password, none)                                                                                                                     | E-mail link, read-only                                                                        |
